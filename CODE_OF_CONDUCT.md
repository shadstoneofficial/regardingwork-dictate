# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Discuss ideas and code without
personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or unwanted
disclosure of another person's private information.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback
- Assuming good intent while addressing real impact
- Respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints
- Correcting mistakes openly and helping others recover from them

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and
publishing private recordings, transcripts, credentials, or personal data are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not use a
public issue when a report contains sensitive or identifying information.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository discussions and in other spaces where
someone is representing the project.
